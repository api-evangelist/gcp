Key highlights include the general availability of Airflow 3.1, new ODBC and JDBC drivers for BigQuery, Conversational Analytics capabilities, and enhancements to Bigtable and Firestore. The update also features new AI-powered tools across BigQuery, Looker, and Cloud SQL to help data teams move faster and build more intelligent pipelines.
