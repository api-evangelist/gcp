An overview of Google's accelerator program supporting global startups, reporting 2,011 startups across 88 countries, 144 cohorts over 10 years, a 93% portfolio survival rate, and $46.3B in funding raised against a $135.1B collective valuation. It highlights initiatives including the Google DeepMind Accelerator in Europe and an alumni community of 1,750+ startups, with open applications for Southeast Asia, China, and Google.org Accelerator programs.
